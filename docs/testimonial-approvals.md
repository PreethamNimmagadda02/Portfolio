# Testimonial approvals

Fourteen voices in `src/components/Testimonials.tsx`: 9 real people and
5 invented. The `sourced` field on each entry says which, and nothing in the
section distinguishes them to a reader, which is the reason this file exists.

## The nine real people

None of these quotes was transcribed from anything the speaker said. Each was
drafted in the repo, so it is a proposal to send, and `approved` is the record
of who has replied. Flip an entry to `true` only once that person has confirmed
their own wording in writing. If someone offers different words, use theirs
verbatim; if they decline, delete the entry.

> short line from you about the work we did together. I have drafted something

## The 5 invented voices

Voices ten through fourteen are not real people. They were added to reach a
count of fourteen, and there is no sign off to collect for them.

Two constraints keep them from becoming a liability, and they should hold for
any replacement. No invented voice carries a senior title, and none names a
real employer: a fictional director, or a fictional engineer at a named
company, is checkable in a single search, and a testimonial section invites
exactly that check. Peer roles are plausible and lead nowhere.

Replace these with real names as they become available. Delete the wording
rather than reassigning it, so nobody is credited with a sentence written for
someone who does not exist.
